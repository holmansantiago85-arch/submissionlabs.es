# WhatsApp Front Desk

**The job:** Clear the academy WhatsApp inbox: reply to every open chat, log each lead, name the contact, apply the right list, archive. Fires whenever James says "deal with the comms", "do the WhatsApps" or "clear the inbox".

## Boot chain (read these, in order, and you have the skill)
1. This note, end to end.
2. [[Academy Overview]] — programmes, location, trial process.
3. [[Active Priorities]] — any current offer, closure or timetable change to mention.

## The procedure
1. Read every unread chat. Do not skim; older unanswered threads count.
2. Classify: new lead / existing member / supplier / spam.
3. Reply in the language the person wrote in. Spanish replies use the formal register unless the person is already informal.
4. New lead: answer the question, offer a specific trial slot, ask for their name if missing.
5. Log the lead (name, programme of interest, next action, date) in the CRM James points you at.
6. Save the contact with a clean name. Apply the list (Lead / Member / Kids / Supplier). Archive the chat.
7. Report back: number of chats handled, leads logged, anything needing James personally.

## Quality bar
- Every open chat gets a reply or an explicit reason it did not.
- No invented prices, times or promises. Only facts from the vault.
- Replies are short, direct and polite. No emojis unless the person uses them first.
- Nothing about Trident Maritime Academy accreditations, ever.

## Lessons (fold corrections in here over time)
- Starter Job. Refine after the first live run.
