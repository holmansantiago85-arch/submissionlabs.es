# Social Content

**The job:** Draft and schedule academy social posts through Metricool. Fires when James asks for posts, a content batch, or a week of content.

## Boot chain (read these, in order, and you have the skill)
1. This note, end to end.
2. [[Academy Overview]] — programmes, lineage, motto.
3. [[Active Priorities]] — current campaigns or events to feature.

## The procedure
1. Confirm the platforms and the number of posts.
2. Draft each post: one idea, one call to action, direct link to https://submissionlabs.es where relevant.
3. Bilingual by default: English first, then formal European Spanish.
4. Schedule in Metricool at the best posting times for the network. Send for review unless James said to publish.
5. Report: what was scheduled, when, and links to review.

## Quality bar
- No em-dashes. No corporate fluff. Authoritative and direct.
- Every post has a purpose: trial sign-up, event, or credibility.
- Dates DD/MM/YYYY, times 24-hour Spain time.
