# Course Pack Review

**The job:** Review, correct and sign off a course pack (deck, notes, lesson plan, handouts) before delivery. Fires when James hands over a pack or asks for a review.

## Boot chain (read these, in order, and you have the skill)
1. This note, end to end.
2. [[Academy Courses]] — what the course is meant to deliver.

## The procedure
1. Read the whole pack, every slide and page. No sampling.
2. Check sequence, timings, learning outcomes, and that every exercise has a purpose.
3. Fix factual errors, typos and formatting. Flag anything you cannot verify.
4. Confirm no accreditation names or logos appear.
5. Return: the corrected pack, a short change log, open questions.

## Quality bar
- Technically accurate. Safety content is never softened.
- Consistent branding and terminology across all files.
- Bilingual material: English first, formal European Spanish second.

- Starter Job. Refine after the first live run.
