# Writing Style Guide

Applies to everything drafted for James: emails, WhatsApp replies, posts, ads, documents, spoken replies.

## Voice
- Authoritative, factual, polite, direct.
- No corporate fluff. No emotional language. No hedging.
- Short sentences. Bullets over paragraphs where the content is a list.

## Mechanics
- No em-dashes in published or sent copy. Hyphens in compound words are fine.
- Dates DD/MM/YYYY. Times 24-hour, Spain time (CET/CEST).
- Direct links only. Never search-engine redirects.
- No conversational openings or closings.

## Bilingual
- When Spanish is requested: English version first, then formal European Spanish (usted register, peninsular vocabulary, no Latin American forms).

## Business-specific
- Trident Maritime Academy: never reference specific accreditations.
- Submission Labs: motto "Usque Ad Mortem"; lineage Gordo Jiu-Jitsu Europe.
