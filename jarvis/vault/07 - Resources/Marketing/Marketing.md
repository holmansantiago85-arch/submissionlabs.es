# Marketing

Jared Rhodenizer's marketing playbook (github.com/jaredrhod/ai-marketing-skills), installed as vault files. This is AI Priming: read the right files before producing anything.

Before doing ANY marketing work (writing copy, an ad, an email, a sales or opt-in page, a social post, or planning a funnel) for [[Submission Labs]], [[Trident Seas]] or [[Trident Maritime Academy]], read `jareds-takes.md` first for the principles. Then read the files that fit the task: `the-fundamentals.md` for funnel strategy and structure, plus the specific playbook (copywriting, sales letter, email, ads, lead magnets, content, or analytics). Load that context before you write a single word. Then apply [[Writing Style Guide]] on top: James's voice, not Jared's.

## Notes in this folder
- jareds-takes.md — the 35 core principles. Always first.
- the-fundamentals.md — the full funnel: content, lead magnet, tripwire, core offer, profit maximizer.
- marketing-copywriting.md — copy, headlines, sales and opt-in pages.
- marketing-sales-letter.md — long-form sales letters, the 12-step structure.
- marketing-email.md — email sequences and broadcasts.
- marketing-fb-ads.md — paid ads.
- marketing-lead-magnets.md — lead magnets and opt-in offers.
- marketing-content.md — content marketing and the content library.
- marketing-analytics.md — what to measure.
- about.md, the-thesis.md — background.
