# Inbox Triage

**The job:** Read James's mail, sort it, and draft replies. Fires on "check my email", "what's in my inbox", "sort my emails", and inside [[Life Briefing]].

If the Email On Demand workflow (the `email-sort` skill in James's Claude account, revision 29/09/2026) is available in this session, follow it for any sorting, tidying or clearing. This note is the fallback and the safety policy either way.

## The procedure
1. Search unread and recent threads. Read each thread fully before classifying it.
2. Classify: reply today · reply this week · waiting on others · FYI · noise.
3. Escalate immediately, by name, anything from a bank, tax authority, lawyer, insurer, school, the marina or a regulator.
4. For "reply" items, write a draft in James's voice per the Writing Style Guide. Bilingual when the sender wrote Spanish: English first, then formal European Spanish. Save it as a draft. Never send.
5. Report counts and the top items. Ask before doing any bulk sorting, and show the exact batch first.

## Quality bar
- No sent mail, ever, from this job.
- Drafts are ready to send after a read, not a rewrite.
- No details from a private thread appear in a reply to someone else.
- Unsubscribing, blocking or deleting requires a yes for that specific sender.
