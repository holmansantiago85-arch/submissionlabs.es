# Bilingual Draft

**The job:** Produce any outbound text in English first, then formal European Spanish. Fires whenever James asks for something "in Spanish", "bilingual", or "for the Spanish clients".

## Boot chain (read these, in order, and you have the skill)
1. This note, end to end.
2. [[Writing Style Guide]] — voice and mechanics.

## The procedure
1. Write the English version to the style guide. Get the facts right first.
2. Translate into formal European Spanish. Usted register. Peninsular vocabulary.
3. Keep names, prices, dates and links identical in both versions.
4. Output: English block, then a rule, then the Spanish block. Nothing else.

## Quality bar
- The Spanish reads as written by a native professional in Spain, not as a translation.
- No em-dashes. No filler. Same information in both versions.

## Lessons (fold corrections in here over time)
- Starter Job. Refine after the first live run.
